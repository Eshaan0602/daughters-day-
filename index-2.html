Happy Daughter's Day, to everyone's little princess.

I wish I could sit next to you while you read this.

Maybe today didn't feel the way a day meant to celebrate you should have felt. Maybe it passed quietly, like any other day. So before it ends, there's something I need you to hear — something you should never have to doubt.

You are loved.

You are so, so loved.

Not just by me. By more people than you probably stop to count.

You are a daughter — someone's little girl, even now, even on the days that feel ordinary.

You are a friend — the one people call first with good news, and the one they call at 2am when something falls apart.

You are someone's favorite person to sit next to, someone's reason to laugh harder than they meant to, someone a stranger remembers because you were kind to them on a hard day.

You have probably been someone's reason to keep going more times than you'll ever know.

Even if you're only mine, I can't help how wonderful you are — how you make the world better just by being in it, without even realizing it. Sometimes it feels like something divine came back down to earth, except this time it's living quietly inside you.

You deserve to be celebrated — not because there's a day on the calendar for daughters, but simply because you are you.

Somewhere in this world there's a girl whose existence has become such a huge part of my life. That still amazes me.

You became someone I worry about, someone I miss, someone I want to protect. Someone whose smile can quietly change my whole day.

Sometimes I wish you could see yourself the way I see you, even just for a moment.

Maybe then you'd understand why I think you deserve so much love.

You deserve softness.

You deserve patience.

You deserve to be held when you're tired.

You deserve someone reminding you it will be okay.

You deserve to feel wanted. Important. Special.

And I hope I get to be one of the people who gives you that, again and again.

Your family loves you more than they probably say out loud. You make this world a little better just by being in it — gentler, warmer, more worth showing up for.

There's something almost sacred about the way you hold your family together, even without trying. And there's something sacred about the way you've made me feel, too — like something in me got quieter and better the day I met you.

You are pure in a way that's rare. Not perfect — pure. The kind of good that doesn't perform, it just exists.

I'd tell you on a random Tuesday. At 3 in the morning. In the middle of an argument. On the happiest day of my life, or the worst day of yours.

I'd still tell you.

You are my baby.

And I hope that no matter how old you get, no matter how much life changes, some quiet part of you always knows — you deserve to be loved gently.

Thank you for existing.

Thank you for being exactly who you are.

And thank you for letting me love you.

Happy Daughter's Day, my pretty girl.

When you close this page, I hope you carry one thing with you.

You are loved more than you know. And you always will be.

I love you, babyyy.
